# XProject — Bartender Cheat Sheet

**One page. Print it. Tape it next to the POS.**

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open XProject on the tablet
2. Tap the **Bartender** card
3. Enter your email + password (your manager has these)
4. You'll land on the dashboard for **your bar only**

If the login fails twice, tell your manager — don't keep trying.

---

## 2. What the dashboard shows

- **Revenue card** — total sold tonight at your bar
- **Stock cards** — every product, with current quantity left
- **Alerts column** — anything red is urgent, anything yellow is a heads-up
- **Chat window** — your manager and the owner can message you here

You can **not** see other bars. You can **not** see other bartenders' chats.

---

## 3. Scanner — your 2 actions

Tap the **Scanner** button at the bottom, then point the tablet camera at a barcode.

| Action | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **INSPECT** | "I want to check what's left of this product" — just looks, doesn't change anything |
| **CONSUMED** | "This bottle is empty / this case is empty" — tells the system to deduct stock |

**Important — CONSUMED is one-way.** If you scan CONSUMED by mistake, tell your manager. Do not scan it twice trying to undo it.

---

## 4. Reading alerts

Alerts appear in the right column on your screen.

| Color | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Red — CRITICAL** | Stock will run out in <45 minutes | Tell your manager NOW, in person or via chat |
| 🟡 **Yellow — WARNING** | Stock low, plan a restock | Mention to manager at next quiet moment |
| 🟢 **Green — OK** | Everything healthy | Nothing |

If you see the same red alert for more than 10 minutes with no manager response, escalate by chat to the owner. The chat goes through immediately.

---

## 5. Chat etiquette

- Be brief — the manager has 4 bars to watch
- Use product names, not codenames: "Bacardi 1L is at 2 bottles" not "the rum"
- If you broke something, say so — phantom sales hurt the platform's accuracy more than honesty does

---

## 6. If the system freezes / shows red banner

1. **Don't panic — keep pouring drinks.** The cash side is on the POS, not on this app
2. Tell your manager
3. Manager will call the XProject team if needed

The POS handles money. XProject is only telling us what's running out — it can be down for a few minutes without anyone losing a sale.

---

## 7. End of shift

- Do one final **INSPECT** scan on the products you're worried about — gives the owner accurate end-of-night numbers
- Log out — tap your avatar (top right) → **Logout**
- Hand the tablet to your replacement or to the manager
