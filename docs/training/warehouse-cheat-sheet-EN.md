# XProject — Warehouse Staff Cheat Sheet

**You are responsible for what enters and leaves the central warehouse during the event. This one page tells you exactly what to scan and when.**

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open XProject on the warehouse tablet
2. Tap the **Warehouse** card
3. Enter your email + password (the owner provides these)
4. You'll land on the scanner view

You will **not** see bar revenue, alerts, or chat. Your view is scanner-only by design — keeps you focused on physical inventory.

---

## 2. The 3 actions you do

| Action | When to use it | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **INTAKE** | A new delivery arrives at the warehouse (truck, supplier drop-off) | + warehouse inventory |
| **DISPATCH** | A manager comes to collect product for a bar | - warehouse inventory, + that bar's stock |
| **RETURN** | A manager brings unsold product back from a bar | + warehouse inventory, - that bar's stock |

**Always scan before moving product, not after.** If you move 6 cases of beer to a bar and then scan, the system shows the wrong location for those 5-10 minutes.

---

## 3. The golden rule

> **Every physical movement of stock = one scan. Every scan = one physical movement.**

If you can't scan (barcode damaged, scanner won't read), tell the owner immediately. Do **not** "make a mental note to scan it later" — you will forget, and that's how phantom stock appears at midnight.

---

## 4. Scanner mechanics

1. Tap the action button (INTAKE / DISPATCH / RETURN)
2. Tap **Scan barcode**
3. Point the tablet at the product's barcode — it auto-detects
4. Enter the quantity (e.g. 6 for a case of 6 bottles, or 1 for one item)
5. For DISPATCH: pick the destination bar from the dropdown
6. Tap **Confirm**
7. A green ✅ confirms the scan was recorded

If you see a red ❌ or a banner saying "scan failed", **redo it immediately** before moving the product. Repeated failures = call the manager.

---

## 5. What you should NOT do

- ❌ Do not scan empty bottles for "CONSUMED" — that's a bartender's action, not yours
- ❌ Do not scan with **ADJUSTMENT** unless the owner tells you to
- ❌ Do not log in with someone else's account "to test something"
- ❌ Do not turn off the tablet during the event — keep it on, plugged in if possible

---

## 6. Pre-event ritual (T-60 min)

Before the first guest arrives:

1. Open XProject, log in, confirm you see the scanner
2. Do **one practice INSPECT scan** on any product to confirm the scanner is working
3. Confirm with the owner that the warehouse "starting inventory" is accurate — if not, raise it now, not during the event
4. Plug the tablet in. Leave it on.

---

## 7. End of event

- Do a final inventory walkthrough — INSPECT scan a sample of products to verify physical = system
- Tell the owner if anything is off — even small mismatches help the post-event report
- Log out, plug the tablet back into its dock
